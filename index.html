Reading schedule, VAT, rebates & late charges

Regular customers: 2% rebate within 7 days of meter reading; no rebate or fine on days 8–15; 5% surcharge on days 16–30, 10% on days 31–40, and 25% after day 40. Non-payment through day 60 can lead to disconnection. Special categories and supplementary bills have different rules. NEA collection regulations, sections 7–10.

The 2083 VAT guideline took effect on 1 Shrawan 2083: non-household supply is generally taxed at 13%; household consumption up to 50 units is exempt, with 5% on the taxable consumption above that threshold. The portal does not report account category or taxable base. VAT here is always NEA’s recorded VAT, not a computed 13% addition. IRD electricity VAT guideline.

Base bills may include energy, minimum/service and demand charges, depending on the tariff category. This statement does not separate those amounts. NEA’s published schedule lists NPR 250 for disconnection and NPR 500 for reconnection; these are separate service charges, not amounts automatically added by this app. NEA tariff and service charges.
